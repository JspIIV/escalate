A disputed fact, settled
in minutes, not days.

On UMA's Optimistic Oracle, anybody can assert a fact with a bond. When somebody disputes it, a token vote decides, in two to four days. Escalate plugs Internet Court into UMA's own escalation manager: GenLayer validators read the claim's sources, rule TRUE or FALSE, and the court settles the assertion on UMA's oracle itself. No relayer, no key, nobody in between.

01UMA's oracle, unmodified

OptimisticOracleV3, Finder, Store and whitelists from UMA's repository, built with UMA's own settings and deployed on GenLayer Chain. The court plugs in through UMA's escalation manager interface.

02The exact claim, or nothing

UMA stores only a hash of a claim. The manager accepts a text only if it hashes, with every field UMA stored, to the assertion id. Nobody can have the court judge different words.

03The court settles

When the verdict is final, the court's own contract answers UMA's price request and calls settleAssertion. The bonds move in the same call.
